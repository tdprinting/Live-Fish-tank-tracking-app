# ReefLive — 3D Asset Specification (Blender `bpy` scripts)

All assets are generated by Python scripts in `tools/blender/` run with the headless `bpy` module (`/tmp/claude-0/bpyenv/bin/python tools/blender/build_all.py`). Outputs are committed under `app/src/main/assets/models/` (GLB) and `app/src/main/assets/textures/` (PNG). No manual Blender work; everything must be reproducible from the scripts.

Coordinate frame: world cm, origin at the inside bottom-front-left corner, +X right, +Y up, +Z toward the viewer (glTF is Y-up so export with `export_yup=True` from Blender's Z-up scene: build the scene in Blender with Z up and the exporter converts; script authors must think in "Blender Z = world Y"). Tank interior: 121.9 × 33.0 × 61.0 cm; sand top at 3 cm; water at 56 cm.

Export settings (shared `export_glb(path)` helper): `bpy.ops.export_scene.gltf(filepath, export_format='GLB', export_apply=True, export_yup=True, export_texcoords=True, export_normals=True, export_colors=True, export_materials='EXPORT', export_image_format='AUTO' (PNG), export_animations=False, export_skins=False, export_extras=True, export_tangents=False)`. Textures are packed. Custom data is passed via object/mesh custom properties (→ glTF `extras`): `part` ("body"|"fin"|"eye"|"polyp"|"antenna"|"shell"), `anchor` (name), `species`.

Vertex color channel `Col` (COLOR_0) is **data, not color**: R = subsurface thickness (soft corals) or 0, G = gill/opercular mask (fish), B = fin hinge distance (fins, normalized 0 at base → 1 at tip), A = spine coordinate `u` (0 snout → 1 tail tip). The exporter keeps RGBA.

## 1. Fish and shrimp: shared procedural method (`tools/blender/fishlib.py`)

### 1.1 Loft body
```
build_body(profiles, length_cm, segments=48, ring_verts=20) -> Mesh
  profiles: list of (u, height_ratio, width_ratio, y_center_offset) sampled along u ∈ [0,1]
  For each of 48 stations along +X (u = i/47), create a ring of 20 vertices: ellipse with
  half-height = height_ratio(u) × L/2, half-width = width_ratio(u) × L/2, center shifted by y_center_offset(u) × L
  (ratios interpolated with Catmull-Rom through the profile table). Rings joined into quads; snout and tail capped
  with a 3-ring fan. Then apply Subdivision (1 level, Catmull-Clark) → ~3.8k tris; shade smooth; UV: cylindrical
  (u along length, v around; seam along the belly) so the texture is painted in a flat "unrolled" space.
  Vertex color A = u.  G = opercular mask: 1 inside the ellipse centered at u∈[0.17,0.26], lower half, feathered.
```
Profile tables below are (u, height, width, yOffset) with ratios relative to body length L; `height` is full body depth / L, `width` is thickness / L. Values are derived from the depth:length ratios in 02_fish_species §A.

### 1.2 Fins
`build_fin(points_2d, thickness=0.0, ray_count)`: a flat polygon (triangulated grid, 2-sided material, ~150–300 tris) placed on the body with `extras.part="fin"`; `Col.b` = normalized distance from the base edge (for clamping); `Col.a` = u at the base. Fin texture: a 512×256 RGBA PNG per fin set painted in Python (Pillow): base fin color with alpha 0.55, ray lines every `width/ray_count` with alpha 0.85 and 1 px width, soft edge gradient to alpha 0 over the last 6% of the span, species margin color on the outer 8%.

### 1.3 Eye
UV sphere r = 0.045 L (species override) at the eye position, 2 materials: sclera/iris texture (ring colors per species) and a black pupil cap; `extras.part="eye"`. The eye is a separate mesh so the shader can swivel it (`uEyeYaw`).

### 1.4 Texture painting (`paintlib.py`)
Textures are generated procedurally with Pillow + numpy in the unrolled UV space (1024×512 for body; u horizontal, v vertical, v=0.5 lateral line, v=0 belly seam, v=1 back... i.e. belly at both edges). Elements: solid base, vertical bars (u ranges, soft edges 2–3% of L, slightly narrower at the belly), spots (Poisson disc samples), stripes (v bands), gradient dorsal→ventral, scale pattern overlay (hexagonal noise at 1–2% L, 6% contrast) for scaled species, mucus speck noise. Roughness is a constant per material (not a map) in v1. Normal maps: none for fish.

### 1.5 LOD and export
Each fish GLB contains one node hierarchy: `Root` → `Body` (mesh), `FinDorsal`, `FinAnal`, `FinCaudal`, `FinPectoralL/R`, `FinPelvicL/R`, `EyeL`, `EyeR`. LOD1 is produced by Decimate (ratio 0.35) on the body and 0.5 on fins and exported as a second GLB `*_lod1.glb`. Triangle budgets: body 3.0–4.5k, fins ≤ 1.2k total, eyes 2×96. Fish are modeled at **unit length 1.0 (L = 1 Blender unit = 1 cm × scale at runtime)** facing +X, lateral line at y=0, belly at −y; the runtime scales by `lengthCm`.

### 3.2 Procedural rock method (`tools/blender/rockscape.py`)
1. **Blocking**: for each element R1–R8 create 2–6 metaballs (`bpy.data.metaballs`, resolution 1.0 cm, threshold 0.6) positioned/scaled per the table (the script holds a literal list of ellipsoid metaballs: center, radii, rotation). Negative metaballs carve the caves/arch openings (R1 cavern, R4 opening, R6 arch, R5 den tunnel: a chain of 4 negative balls r 4 cm along the den path). Convert to mesh.
2. **Remesh** voxel 1.2 cm; **Displace** ×2: Voronoi F1 (scale 6 cm, strength 1.5 cm) for lumps, Musgrave/fBm (scale 1.5 cm, 4 octaves, strength 0.5 cm) for pits; a third displace with a Voronoi "crackle" (scale 2 cm, strength −0.3 cm) for pores.
3. **Boolean holes**: scatter 150 spheres r 0.6–1.5 cm on the surface (random faces, weighted toward upward and front-facing faces) and boolean-difference them (pores up to 3 cm look). Dedicated holes: blenny hole (r 1.3 cm, 6 cm deep at the R2 position), 5 small crevices.
4. **Decimate** to 40k tris (planar + collapse), smooth shading with 45° auto-smooth.
5. **UV**: Smart UV Project (angle 66°, margin 0.002) into one 2048² atlas.
6. **Bake** (Cycles, 64 samples, CPU) albedo + AO + normal + roughness from a procedural material: base `#D8CBB0` tan with `#B9A98C` crevices (AO-driven), **green film** `#5E6B38` mix 0.5 on upward-facing areas with noise mask (this tank's rock reads green-brown), **coralline** patches `#D46A8A`/`#7F5CB5` (noise mask threshold 0.72, mostly on R1 right half, R6 and R8), **hair algae** `#3B5B2E` mask stored in the albedo alpha channel (so the shader can scale it with `uAlgaeBloom`), white `#E8E2D4` on R3's upper half and R5's front (fresh-looking rock in the photos). Roughness 0.85 ± 0.1 by noise. Output: `rock_albedo.png` 2048², `rock_normal.png` 1024², `rock_rough_ao.png` 1024² (R = roughness, G = AO).
7. Export `rockscape.glb` (single mesh node `Rock`, extras `anchor` list serialized separately).

### 3.3 Sand — part of `tank.glb`
Grid 120×32 (1 cm quads), heightmap: base 3 cm + 1.5 cm mounds against rock feet (distance-to-rock based) − 0.5 cm in the lanes + fBm 0.3 cm; 2k tris after decimate. Texture: `sand_albedo.png` 1024² tiled 4×: off-white `#E6DFD0`/`#C9BEA6` grain noise with sparse darker specks `#A89C86` and a few brown detritus flecks; green film `#8FA060` mask at 8% along the front edge (y < 1 cm from the front glass, matching the photo's algae line); `sand_normal.png` 512² fine grain. Roughness 0.9.

### 3.5 SDF bake (`tools/sdf/bake_sdf.py`)
Loads `rockscape.glb` with `trimesh` (pip) or via bpy's `BVHTree.FromObject`; grid 1 cm over the tank interior; signed distance via `BVHTree.find_nearest` + inside test (ray parity); cover score = fraction of 26 directions (hemisphere up + 8 horizontal + 8 oblique down) hitting rock within 20 cm. Writes `rock_sdf.bin` (SIMULATION §5.2). Runtime < 2 min.

### 6. Textures: naming and budgets
`textures/<asset>_albedo.png` (sRGB), `_normal.png`, `_rough_ao.png`; fish 1024×512 body + 512×256 fins; rock 2048/1024/1024; sand 1024/512; corals 512; `caustic_512.png`, `water_n1.png`, `water_n2.png` (512² tileable normal maps from fBm), `room_env.png` 256×128, `irid_lut.png` 64×1. Total asset budget ≤ 28 MB in the APK (PNG). The Blender scripts write PNGs via `image.save_render` or Pillow; never JPEG.

### 7. Species 2D art for the app
`tools/blender/render_cards.py`: for each fish GLB, render a 512² PNG with a 3/4 side view, toon-ish lighting (3-point, no shadows, transparent background) → `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/species_<id>.webp` (convert with Pillow, quality 85).

### 8. Build order and verification
`build_all.py` runs: `fishlib` self-test → each species → `rockscape` → `corals` → `tank` → `render_cards` → `bake_sdf`. It prints per-asset triangle counts and fails if any exceeds its budget by > 15%. A `preview.py` renders a 1280×720 Eevee shot of the assembled scene from the front (camera at (61, 35, 140) cm looking at (61, 28, 16)) to `docs/preview/scene.png` for visual review against `tank_full.jpg`. Reviewer compares: pillar positions, cave openings, Kenya position/size, GSP mat footprint, GSP neon clump height, colors under blue-white light.
