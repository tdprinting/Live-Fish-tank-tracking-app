# Source pack for livestock research and reef simulation

Curated 2026-10-12. This is a discovery guide for Astra, not a set of tank recommendations. Open the linked source, then cite the specific paper, species record, dataset version, or article used. **Do not turn ocean averages into aquarium targets, or species-family observations into species-specific rules without labeling the inference.** A site's inclusion does not validate every claim on it.

| Source | Use |
| --- | --- |

| [Journal of Experimental Marine Biology and Ecology](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-experimental-marine-biology-and-ecology) | Experiments on marine organisms and environmental responses. |

| [PLOS ONE: Marine and Aquatic Sciences](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/browse/Marine_and_Aquatic_Sciences) | Open-access original studies across marine taxa. |
| [Journal of Fish Biology](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/10958649) | Fish physiology, ecology, life history, and behavior. |
| [ICES Journal of Marine Science](https://academic.oup.com/icesjms) | Marine populations, ecosystems, and environmental response. |
| [Limnology and Oceanography](https://www.aslo.org/limnology-and-oceanography/) | Ocean chemistry, microbes, nutrients, and productivity. |
| [Animal Behaviour](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/animal-behaviour) | Behavior experiments, including fish and invertebrates. |
| [Aquatic Biology](https://www.int-res.com/journals/ab) | Marine and freshwater organismal biology. |
| [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) | Broad paper discovery; search scientific name plus a process or behavior. |
| [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) | Life-science literature discovery, useful for disease and physiology. |

Search indexes are **not** evidence by themselves. Read the actual paper and note its study species, life stage, environment, sample size, result, and uncertainty. Some journal articles are paywalled.

## 6. Modeling approaches

| Source | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [Ecopath with Ecosim](https://ecopath.org/) | Food-web and ecosystem modeling concepts. |
| [NOAA ecosystem modeling](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/pacific-islands/ecosystems/ecosystem-modeling-pacific) | Examples of coupled ecological drivers and model validation. |
| [Coral Trait Database methods](https://www.coraltraits.org/procedures) | Provenance for quantitative coral traits and observation context. |
| [NOAA Coral Reef Watch product methods](https://coralreefwatch.noaa.gov/product/index.php) | Define thermal stress variables and spatial/temporal scales. |

Ecosystem models are useful design references; they do not directly specify individual-fish behavior in an aquarium. Record each proposed simulation rule as an evidence-backed rule, a cautious analogy, or a game-design assumption.

## Astra research instructions

For every livestock species in the project, make a profile with: accepted scientific name and identification confidence; diet and feeding mode; natural habitat; adult size and movement; daily activity; social and territorial behavior; interactions with fish, invertebrates, corals, and algae; reproduction; aquarium care observations; stress or disease signs; and unresolved questions. For every parameter or simulation rule, record the **exact source URL/DOI, studied species and environment, measured result, confidence, and transfer caveat**. Separate wild-reef findings, aquarium observations, and design assumptions. Where evidence conflicts, preserve both claims and explain why.
