# Hermit crab anatomy and motion (Clibanarius tricolor, Paguristes cadenati)

Used by `tools/blender/inverts.py` `build_hermit` and `core/sim/LimbRig.kt` `HermitLegs` (SIMULATION §19.5).
[L] = inferred from general decapod knowledge, no species source found.

## Size, shells, colour
- Blue-leg hermit, *C. tricolor*: up to 2-2.5 cm; prefers Cerithium shells, up to ~3.8 cm. Legs blue with red bands between segments; legs "blue, orange and yellow banded"; antennae bright orange. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clibanarius_tricolor), [Reefguide](https://www.reefguide.org/clibanariustricolor.html), [mexican-crabs](https://mexican-crabs.org/blue-leg-hermit-crab/). Chelae near equal ("equal handed hermit"). Eyestalk colour not sourced: orange-tan with a pale ring under the cornea [L].
- Scarlet hermit, *P. cadenati*: ~3 cm, bright red, smooth and hairless limbs; long yellow eyestalks; yellowish-green corneas; deep red antennae; left chela slightly larger; juveniles mostly in Cerithium and Vasum shells. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paguristes_cadenati), [Reeflex](https://www.reeflex.net/tiere/887_Paguristes_cadenati.htm).
- Outside the shell: chelipeds and two walking-leg pairs (P2-P3); P4-P5 are reduced and grip the shell; the soft abdomen stays coiled inside [L, standard paguroid anatomy]. Eyestalk ≈ shield length, antennal flagellum ≈ 2.5 shield lengths [L].

## Motion
- Walking: forward, alternating diagonal stepping of the walking legs, chelipeds mainly support (*Coenobita compressus*, Herreid & Full 1986, J. Exp. Biol. 120:283-296, abstract). Shell load did not change step frequency or stride length (same study). Stride 0.3 shell lengths, 40 % swing [L].
- Shell carriage: shell drags or is lifted just clear, rolling with each shift of load [L]. Congener *C. erythropus* moves slowly overall, 0.7 cm/min ([abstract](https://iris.uniss.it/handle/11388/134355)); the sim's stop-go walk (1.5 cm/s bursts with pauses, MotionProfile) is unchanged.
- Antennules flick in bursts followed by pauses; flicking raises odour capture ~21 % on the downstroke (*Coenobita rugosus*, Waldrop & Koehl 2016, J. R. Soc. Interface). Rate 2.5 Hz [L].
- Claws: alternate strokes to the mouth while picking film and detritus, 1.2 Hz each [L].
- Withdrawal: the body is pulled into the shell, the large cheliped and front walking legs seal the aperture, hooked rear legs anchor (Australian Museum Records vol. 15). Snap-in ≈ 0.15 s, slow re-emergence eyestalks first [L].
