# Microfauna raw source facts (compiled 2026-10-12)

Conventions: "NF" = not found in the sources checked this session. Forum or retailer-only claims are marked FORUM. Units are kept as published; where a source has a typo or an internal conflict, that is noted. "ppt" = ‰ as written in the source.

## 1. Tigger pods (Parvocalanus crassirostris)

- Adult size (mm): NF. Species pages give no lengths. https://copepodes.obs-banyuls.fr/en/fichesp.php?sp=1090
- Generation/development time: NF in fetched sources.
- Culture densities (Ourgern et al. 2026, Thai J. Agric.): peak 3.33 ± 0.51 ind/mL on day 2 with Isochrysis galbana; 3.44 ± 0.56 ind/mL on day 4 with I. galbana + Chaetoceros sp.; 3 ± 0.19 ind/mL (fermented rice bran); 2.44 ± 0.11 ind/mL (fermented soybean meal). https://li01.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/agkasetkaj/article/view/266841
- Culture period: 8.00 d with algae diet vs 18 ± 2.31 d (rice bran) and 19.33 ± 2.67 d (soybean meal). Same URL.
- Salinity trial (same paper; temperature not in abstract): 28 ppt max density 5.67 ± 0.38 ind/mL; 30 ppt 3.33 ± 0.33; 32 ppt 4.22 ± 0.49; 35 ppt 3.33 ± 0.33; 28 ppt reported as most significant (p<0.01). "Growth value" is labelled "days" but undefined. Same URL.
- Acartia vs Parvocalanus demographics (conference abstract): 27.5 °C; salinities 20, 25, 30, 35 ppt; fed live Tisochrysis lutea above estimated carbon saturation (1,000 g C/L for P. crassirostris). Daily egg production fell significantly over 7 d (p<0.001). Salinity had no effect on P. crassirostris fecundity. https://bbs20.dryfta.com/abstract-archive/abstract/public/455/the-effect-of-salinity-on-population-demographics-of-the-copepods-acartia-tonsa-and-parvocalanus-crassirostris
- Lab culture conditions cited (Alajmi & Zeng 2015, via Choi et al. 2021): 26 °C, 16L:8D, T-iso food. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2021.704479/full
- Eggs per female, diet in wild, day/night behaviour, copper tolerance: NF.
- Reef-tank densities: FORUM/retailer only; no numbers extracted (see section 19).

## 2. Apocyclops panamensis

- Adult size: NF. N1 nauplii 70 x 100 µm. https://www.was.org/MeetingAbstracts/ShowAbstract/45965
- Life cycle: "accomplishes its life cycle in six days" at 28 and 32 °C (the source marks this "data not shown"). Stage-by-stage durations NF. https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/7768379.pdf
- Temperature tested: 24, 28, 32 °C (measured means 24.24, 27.86, 31.75 °C); best at 32 °C. Tolerance described qualitatively ("wide tolerance range"). Same Dialnet PDF.
- Salinity: collected at 6.6 ppt, acclimatised to 30 ppt (cited). Growth best at 28 and 32 ppt; fewer nauplii than expected at 32. Same PDF.
- Population growth: best treatment (32 °C, 28 ppt) grew about 3.4x in 14 d; nauplii 738.09 ± 602.15 ind/L at end. Abstract gives 1,380.95 ± 1,267.06 ind/L; Results give 1,502.00 ± 457.74 ind/L (unexplained conflict). About 11,600 copepods/L reached in a 1,000 L tank at 28 and 32 °C. Same PDF.
- Eggs/clutch per female, diet, day/night, copper tolerance: NF.
- Congener A. dengizicus (for scale only, not A. panamensis): N1 75 x 120 µm; egg-to-adult 7.4 d at 35 °C; population doubling time as short as 2.2 d; fecundity up to 647 nauplii per female. https://www.was.org/MeetingAbstracts/ShowAbstract/45965

## 3. Tisbe (harpacticoid copepods)

- Species: Tisbe furcata (Bergmans 1981, J. Mar. Biol. Ass. UK 61:691-705). Only species covered by this source.
- Stage durations at 18 °C: combined nauplii 80.4 h; copepodites I-III 23.0, 23.2, 28.0 h (females); IV 33.4 h (F) / 24.2 h (M); V 48.8 h (F) / 34.7 h (M). https://marineinfo.org/doc/publication/3705
- Life-table parameters (same source, 18 °C): intrinsic rate of increase rm 0.233 d^-1; net reproductive rate R0 94; minimum generation time Tmin 14.9 d; generation time T 19.5 d; mean generation time 16.4 d; Te 25.4 d. Same URL.
- Body size, eggs per female, salinity and temperature tolerance, densities: NF in this source.

## 4. Acartia tonsa

- Egg production rate (EPR), Rio de la Plata field, 2009-2011: individual values 6.5 or 6.7 (source conflict) to 95.7 eggs/female/day. Cruise means 10.6 ± 6.0 (31 May) to 86 ± 9.2 (16 Oct 2009). Lab maximum about 30 eggs/female/day. https://revistas.usp.br/bjoce/en/article/download/164250/157612/380463
- EPR vs salinity: humped; fitted Ricker model a = 9.40 ± 2.84, b = 0.075 ± 0.029 (EPR ~ a·S·exp(-bS)). Maximum at 10-15 (Results) or 10-20 (Discussion). Same URL.
- EPR vs temperature: no relationship found; sampled range 10.54-24.56 °C; salinity sampled 2.83-32.99. Same URL.
- Earlier regional values (cited in same paper): Calliari et al. 2004 mean 6.8 eggs/f/d (range 0.7-7.5); Derisio et al. 2014 mean 8.6 (range 2.4-14.9). Same URL.
- Field vs lab EPR ratio (lowest:highest): 2.2 field, 1.7 lab. Same URL.
- Fecundity vs salinity (conference abstract, 25 °C, 20/25/30/35 ppt, T. lutea food): highest total fecundity at 30 ppt (p=0.02). https://bbs20.dryfta.com/abstract-archive/abstract/public/455/the-effect-of-salinity-on-population-demographics-of-the-copepods-acartia-tonsa-and-parvocalanus-crassirostris
- Peck & Holste 2006 (cited in Choi et al. 2021): highest EPR at 15 psu within 5-30 psu; EPR declines above 20 psu. Holste & Peck 2005 (cited): hatching decreases significantly below 15 psu. Chinnery & Williams 2004 (cited): hatching highest at 20 °C and 15.5 psu. Stottrup et al. 1986 (cited): daily mortality about 5% at optimal salinity, rising about 50% above 25 psu. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2021.704479/full
- Culture conditions cited (Peck & Holste 2006): 18 and 23 °C, 25-30 psu, 12L:12D, Rhodomonas sp. food. Drillet et al. 2011 review: 17 °C, 30 ppt, Rhodomonas salina. Same Frontiers URL.
- Copper: Pinho et al. 2007 (Aquat. Toxicol. 84:62-70), CuCl2; 48 h LC50 cited as previously determined under same conditions. Salinities 5, 15, 30 ppt. Numeric LC50 NF in abstract. Temperature NF. https://repositorio.furg.br/handle/1/449
- Adult length (mm), development time, generation time: NF.
- Related species (not A. tonsa, for scale): A. ohtsukai EPR 1.90 ± 0.40 to 11.10 ± 1.20 eggs/f/d, highest at 25 °C; food 40,000 cells/mL Isochrysis galbana plus 3,000 cells/mL Tetraselmis suecica; tested 10-30 °C, 10-33 psu. A. tsuensis: development egg to adult across 17.5-30 °C, optimal near 25 °C (Takahashi & Ohno 1996, cited). https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2021.704479/full

## 7. Mysid shrimp

- Species: Mysidopsis californica (only species with data in source). Adult female mean 7.3 mm (SD 0.54); other sections give 6.92 and 7.1 mm. Juvenile length at release 1.7 mm (SD 0.16). https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/449/44918833011.pdf
- Brood size: average 10 juveniles per 7 mm female; females 5.5-8.5 mm gave 2-16 juveniles; mean across 406 females 9.96 (SD 4.29) or 10.3 (SD 3.15). Text reports 1.56 juveniles per mm (correlation weak, r=0.27, p=0.197). Same URL.
- Development: generation 1 marsupium 13-17 d, release 6-7 d later; generation 2 marsupium about 12 d, release about 6 d later. Same URL.
- Temperature: 20.5-21.2 °C (gen 1); 21.6-23.3 °C (gen 2); authors suggest 20 ± 2 °C. Salinity 35 ‰ (gen 1), 34 ‰ (gen 2). Same URL.
- Survival: 88% (gen 1), 77.7% (gen 2); mortality 12-23% in discussion. Sex ratio F:M 2.5:1 to 3.0:1. Same URL.
- Food: Artemia nauplii and rotifers, ad libitum. Same URL.
- Densities: brooding females individually in 150 mL; adults in 30 L aquaria; gen 1 juveniles 250 total in three 2 L flasks (about 83 per flask); gen 2 juveniles 36 total (12 per flask). Same URL.
- Across species (Mauchline 1973, cited): female length 1.8-145 mm; brood size 1-245; egg diameter 0.2-4 mm; incubation 8-180 d at about 3-23 °C. Same URL.
- Copper, day/night behaviour, reef-tank densities: NF.

## 10. Rotifers (Brachionus plicatilis)

- Life table at 15 / 20 / 25 °C (FAO, after Ruttner-Kolisko 1972, Table 3.1):
  - Embryonic development 1.3 / 1.0 / 0.6 d.
  - Age at first spawning 3.0 / 1.9 / 1.3 d.
  - Interval between spawnings 7.0 / 5.3 / 4.0 h.
  - Lifespan 15 / 10 / 7 d.
  - Eggs per female over life 23 / 23 / 20.
  - https://www.fao.org/4/w3732e/w3732e0h.htm
- Body length in µm: not stated on the FAO page. Glavic et al. 2000 (poster) measured lorica: 9 combinations of 38, 27, 20 psu x 35, 27, 22 °C; 50 animals per condition; Nannochloropsis sp. about 4 x 10^6 ± 1 x 10^6 cells/mL; 400 L tanks. Lorica 89.50% under 150 µm at 38 psu and 27 °C; 53.87% in 150-250 µm at 20 psu and 22 °C; 58.43% in 150-250 µm at 27 psu and 22 °C (ANOVA F=46.757, p<0.001). Source text garbled in places. https://www.bib.irb.hr/46201
- Salinity: tolerates 1-97 ppt; optimal reproduction below 35 ppt. Stock culture 25 ppt; Oceanic Institute system 23 ppt. Same FAO URL.
- Temperature: stock 28 ± 1 °C; upscaling not above 30 °C; Culture Selco protocol 25 °C. No tolerance range given for B. plicatilis on this page (15-31 °C figure applies to freshwater B. calyciflorus and B. rubens). Same FAO URL.
- Culture densities (FAO):
  - Stock culture: 2 rotifers/mL rising to about 200/mL after one week.
  - Upscale: stocked at 50/mL, reaching about 200/mL in 3 d; harvest at 200-300/mL.
  - Mass production on algae: starter 50-150/mL. Japanese large systems: 80-200/mL.
  - Culture Selco system: 200/mL to about 600/mL after 4 d.
  - Continuous high-density culture: averages about 1000/mL, peaks above 3000/mL.
  - Egg ratio about 30% at 150/mL, about 10% at 2000/mL, below 5% at 5000/mL. Same FAO URL.
- Water quality (FAO): minimum dissolved oxygen 2 mg/L; best above pH 7.5 (lives above 6.6); un-ionised ammonia below 1 mg/L. Same FAO URL.
- Algae for rotifers (FAO): marine Chlorella 1.6 x 10^6 cells/mL for upscaling; concentrates 1-2 x 10^8 cells/mL. Nannochloropsis 13-14 x 10^6 cells/mL in Oceanic Institute batch system; described as the usual marine alga for mass production. Same FAO URL.
- Copper tolerance, day/night behaviour: NF.
- PMC5893227 ("The effects of microalgae as live food for Brachionus plicatilis in intensive culture system") was blocked by a verification page in the proxy; not used.

## 13. Tetraselmis

- Tetraselmis marina AC16-MESO: cells ellipsoid, 15-20 µm length, 5-10 µm width; genus range cited as 10-20 µm. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10574681
- Max f/2 culture density: "8.7 x 10^6 cells/mL" in source, likely 10^6 (typo in source). Inoculum 2.5 x 10^5 cells/mL (growth curve); 3 x 10^5 cells/mL (salinity assays, likely). Same URL.
- Salinity assay peaks: 2.5 ‰ 6.3 x 10^5 cells/mL; 5 ‰ 1.1 x 10^6 cells/mL; 15 ‰ 8.6 x 10^5 cells/mL. Tested 0.6-120 ‰; optimum 5-15 ‰ per authors. 20 ± 1 °C. Same URL.
- Productivity: 0.2 g/L/d mid-exponential, 0.03 g/L/d late stationary. Same URL.
- T. suecica salinity optimum for biomass: 20-60 ‰ (cited in same source). Same URL.
- Copepod feed: Tetraselmis suecica 3,000 cells/mL (A. ohtsukai study, see section 4). https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2021.704479/full

## 14. Bristleworms

- Eurythoe complanata (SeaLifeBase): max length blank; max published weight 12.54 g; depth 1-1100 m; preferred temperature 7.4-15.2 °C (mean 10.1 °C, n = 611 records). Diet: voracious carnivore (sponges, hydroids, coral polyps, ascidians; UAE record). Fragmentation 10-15 min; blastema about 1 week; larval development 88 h (ciliated larvae 12 h, trochophore 48 h, metatrochophore 72 h). https://www.sealifebase.ca/summary/38154
- Eurythoe salinity, reef densities: NF.
- "Spaghetti worm": taxonomy not resolved by this session. Eunice aphroditois data (Bobbit worm, a large eunicid, not a microfauna proxy): record length 299 cm; typical "less than 10 cm to nearly 3 m"; body width about 25.5 mm; depth up to 95 m; ambush predator, takes fish. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eunice_aphroditois
- Any spaghetti-worm species in the reef hobby still needs ID before use.

## 17. Copper tolerance (cross-group)

- Tigriopus japonicus: 96-h LC50 for Cu = 32.75 mg/L (about 32,750 µg/L), salinities 5, 15, 25, 35 ‰; temperature NF. Paper contrasts with an earlier value of 3.9 mg/L. Marine Pollution Bulletin 85(2):526-531, 2014. https://pure.skku.edu/en/publications/effect-of-salinity-on-acute-copper-and-zinc-toxicity-to-tigriopus/
- Caution: the 32.75 mg/L value is far above typical reef-safety thresholds; treat as a lab LC50 for a species not in the reef-tank list, not a usable tank limit.
- Acartia tonsa: numeric 48 h LC50 not in the abstract (section 4).
- Copper tolerance for Parvocalanus, Apocyclops, Tisbe, mysids, amphipods, ostracods, isopods, rotifers, ciliates, foraminifera: NF in this session.

## 18. Fish consumption of copepods and amphipods

- Sphaeramia orbicularis (Kenyan mangrove; Mees, Mwamsojo & Wakwabi 1999, Biol. Jb. Dodonaea 66:134-145). Note: S. orbicularis, not S. nematoptera.
  - Gammaridean amphipods and tanaids dominant prey in both size classes (stomach content, numerical). https://marineinfo.org/doc/publication/4024
  - Smaller fish (mode about 65 mm SL) supplemented diet with harpacticoid copepods. Same URL.
  - Larger fish (mode about 80 mm SL) also ate postlarval brachyuran crabs and caridean shrimp (by weight). Same URL.
  - Mature gonads in most fish above 40 mm SL; 4,700-10,000 eggs per female. Same URL.
  - Otolith ages about 11 months (smaller cohort) and 15 months (larger cohort). Same URL.
  - Consumption per fish per day, prey size, diel feeding: NF in abstract. Full paper as Open Marine Archive PDF: https://www.vliz.be/imisdocs/publications/ocrd/157659.pdf
- Site-attached fish vs gobies capture (Ella & Genin 2024, Frontiers in Marine Science 10:1327581): feeding rates given as "1-3 min^-1 vs 0.5-2 s^-1" (units inconsistent in the text; verify against original sources). Prey densities used 210 and 630 prey/m^3. Capture efficiency 3-27%. Artemia nauplii 0.60 ± 0.07 mm. Fish described as diurnal; no day/night feeding rates. Copepod consumption: NF (copepods only described as dominant natural taxon, Noda et al. 1992). https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2023.1327581/pdf
- Consumption rates by wrasses, dottybacks, hawkfish, blennies: NF in this session.

## 19. Capture of zooplankton by corals, soft corals and zoanthids

- Saper et al. 2023, Coral Reefs (doi 10.1007/s00338-023-02397-1). Artemia nauplii only; no copepods, rotifers, soft corals or zoanthids tested. Species: Acropora millepora, Pocillopora acuta, Galaxea fascicularis. Temperature 24 °C; 1 h trials. https://researchonline.jcu.edu.au/79208/1/Saper%20et%20al%202023%20Quantifying%20capture%20and%20ingestion%20of%20live%20feeds%20Coral%20reefs.pdf
  - Instar I nauplii (508 ± 45 µm, about 0.5 mm) capture, ind/polyp/h: A. millepora 0.31 ± 0.08, 0.57 ± 0.08, 1.22 ± 0.13 at 1, 2, 4 ind/mL. P. acuta 0.06 ± 0.02, 0.06 ± 0.01, 0.14 ± 0.09 at 1, 2, 4 ind/mL. G. fascicularis 16.15 ± 1.69, 26.16 ± 2.20, 43.67 ± 5.01, 47.87 ± 9.56 at 1, 2, 4, 8 ind/mL. Same URL.
  - Capture vs ingestion (instar II, 735 ± 94 µm, 3 ind/mL): A. millepora capture 0.7 vs ingestion 0.2 ind/polyp/h; P. acuta capture 1 vs ingestion 3.5; G. fascicularis capture 59 vs ingestion 75. Same URL.
  - Cited comparison: G. fascicularis and Duncanopsammia axifuga consume more than 100 Artemia per polyp; small-polyp Acropora about 1 nauplius per 10 polyps at the same densities. Same URL.
- Soft corals and zoanthids on copepods/rotifers: NF in this session. Leads only (not fetched): Madracis mirabilis and Montastrea cavernosa zooplankton capture in a field enclosure (https://acnpsearch.tweb-dev.unibo.it/singlejournalindex/3504215); Desmophyllum dianthus (cold-water coral) functional response on krill and copepods (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6223235/). The latter is not a reef species.
