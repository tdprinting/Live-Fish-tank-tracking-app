# Reef and marine research websites

Curated 2026-10-12. Use this directory to locate sources, then cite the specific species record, dataset, or paper that supports a claim. A wild-reef measurement is context, not automatically a target for an aquarium. A hobby article is useful for husbandry but is not equivalent to a controlled study.

## Scientific papers and discovery

| Website | Best use | Important limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Coral Reefs](https://link.springer.com/journal/338) | Original coral-reef ecology, biogeochemistry, reef organisms, and management studies. Official journal of the International Coral Reef Society. | Some articles require access; check the methods and studied species before applying a result to a tank. |
| [Marine Ecology Progress Series](https://www.int-res.com/journals/meps) | Original studies of marine, coastal, and estuarine ecology, including feeding and behavior. | Search broadly; many papers concern habitats or taxa outside tropical reefs. |
| [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) | Find papers by scientific name plus behavior, diet, salinity, temperature, or disease terms; follow “Cited by” to later work. | A search index, not a quality filter. Results can include theses, preprints, and non-peer-reviewed material. |
| [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) | Find research on marine animal physiology, microbiology, disease, and toxicology when indexed. | Biomedical focus means it does not cover every marine ecology journal. |
| [Crossref Metadata Search](https://search.crossref.org/) | Verify a paper’s DOI, authors, title, and publication details. | Bibliographic metadata is not the full study or a quality judgment. |

## Species names, distributions, and traits

| Website | Best use | Important limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [FishBase](https://www.fishbase.org/) | Fish taxonomy, range, ecology, diet, life history, and cited source records. | The entry’s depth and age vary by species; follow its references for behavioral claims. |
| [SeaLifeBase](https://www.sealifebase.org/) | Marine organisms other than finfish, especially invertebrate ecology and life history. | Coverage varies; verify sparse entries against primary literature. |
| [World Register of Marine Species (WoRMS)](https://www.marinespecies.org/) | Accepted scientific names, synonyms, and taxonomic identity. Start here when a common name is ambiguous. | Primarily a taxonomic authority, not a care guide. |
| [Coral Trait Database](https://www.coraltraits.org/) | Measured coral traits and the observations or studies behind them. | Trait values depend on species and conditions; inspect units, context, and original source. |

## Ocean and reef measurements

| Website | Best use | Important limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [NOAA Coral Reef Watch](https://coralreefwatch.noaa.gov/) | Sea-surface temperature, heat stress, bleaching alerts, and historical reef-scale products. | Satellite conditions are not aquarium measurements. |
| [NOAA Coral Reef Conservation Program](https://coralreef.noaa.gov/) | Reef-monitoring programs, reports, publications, and management research. | Many resources address ecosystem conservation rather than home husbandry. |
| [Australian Institute of Marine Science (AIMS)](https://www.aims.gov.au/research-topics/monitoring-and-discovery/monitoring-great-barrier-reef) | Long-running Great Barrier Reef coral, fish, and water-quality monitoring. | Geography is concentrated in Australian reefs. |
| [Smithsonian Ocean: Corals and Coral Reefs](https://ocean.si.edu/ocean-life/invertebrates/corals-and-coral-reefs) | Scientist-reviewed explanations and entry points into reef ecology. | A synthesis for context; cite underlying studies for a precise species claim. |

## Aquarium chemistry and husbandry

| Website | Best use | Important limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Reef2Reef: Optimal Parameters for a Coral Reef Aquarium](https://www.reef2reef.com/threads/optimal-parameters-for-a-coral-reef-aquarium-by-randy-holmes-farley.173563/) | Detailed, aquarist-oriented explanation of temperature, salinity, alkalinity, calcium, magnesium, nitrate, phosphate, and other parameters. | Expert recommendations, not universal species-specific thresholds; the author has revised some ranges. |
| [Reefs.com: Aquarium Chemistry](https://reefs.com/magazine/aquarium-chemistry-the-carbonate-system-in-the-aquarium-and-the-ocean-part-i-the-components-of-the-carbonate-system/) | In-depth explanations of the carbonate system and how reef-tank chemistry differs from the ocean. | Older articles may need checking against newer research and methods. |

## Suggested research path for this project

1. Resolve each animal’s scientific name in WoRMS; check FishBase or SeaLifeBase for range and ecology.
2. Search that name in Google Scholar, *Coral Reefs*, and *Marine Ecology Progress Series*. Record which observations are direct, from related species, or inferred.
3. Use NOAA and AIMS for natural reef context. Use the chemistry references for aquarium practice. Keep those contexts separate.
4. For each claim carried into project research, save the specific source URL, date, studied taxon, and any condition that limits transfer to a home aquarium.
