# Tee's tank in daylight photos (2026-10-13)

Top-tier evidence: 14 phone photos of Tee's own 60 gal tank plus a full-tank shot, taken 2026-10-13 with the display
lights on (`reference/photos_2026-10-13/p01.jpg`-`p14.jpg`, `reference/tank_full_2026-10-13.jpg`). Cite as `[P p05]`
(photo 5) or `[P full]`. What is **seen** in a photo is **[H]**; an identification carries its own confidence;
care and biology numbers from outside sources are **[M]** (sources at the end); reasoned values are **[L]**.
Companion to the night clips in [tee_tank_night.md](tee_tank_night.md).

Tee's note with the photos: "The green star polyps especially needs some work and there may be some new coral that
needs added in the pictures."

## Sim behaviour added (core)
| Item | Rule | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| `CoralKind.LEATHER` (*Sarcophyton*, *Sinularia*, *Lobophytum*, *Cladiella*) | Light band 0.15-0.95 (moderate, PAR ~100-200); night extension 0.25; needs flow and dislikes a direct jet. Water-model biomass 0.05 (small: the water chemistry was tuned on Tee's tank, which already held these corals) | M1, M2, M4 [M]; biomass [L] |
| Wax shedding | Every 24 days a leather closes (extension x0.1) for 3 days, 6 days if flow is weak; phase per colony | M3, M1 [M] |
| Growth | Tree form, 0.4 %/day at full vigour (x4 a year), cap 2.5 x | M1 [M] ("a 5 cm frag can reach about 20 cm across within a year") |
| Allelopathy | Terpene pressure on neighbours within 15 cm (0.25 x vigour, carbon cuts 60 %), allelochemical source 0.003 / h per colony | M4, M5, M6 [M] |
| Plankton | Leather polyps count as 80 small particle feeders | [L] |
| Elephant ear | `MUSHROOM` kind (genus `amplexidiscus` added); low flow, medium light | M7, M8 [M] |
| GSP | Same `GSP` kind; long-polyp form only changes the model and polyp animation (polyps longer under dimmer light, M9) | [P p12], M9 [M] |
