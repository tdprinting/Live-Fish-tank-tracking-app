# Reef Tank Research: Corals, Macroalgae, Live Rock, Lighting, Water Rendering

Scope: 60 gal reef (48" long), 2x AI Prime HD on goosenecks. ### A3. Care-relevant mm sizes and polyp counts (use for LOD / instance counts)

- Kenya tip branch: use 300-800 polyp instances per 10 cm colony at full extension. **[E]**
- GSP mat: 5x5 cm patch = ~400-800 polyps at 1.5-2 mm spacing. **[E]**
- Zoa colony 10 cm across: ~150-300 polyps. **[E]**
- Clove tuft (5 cm): ~50-120 polyps. **[E]**

## E. Live rock and aquascaping

### E1. Quantity

| Metric | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Live rock | 1-1.5 lb per gal; smaller tanks toward high end; less if sump or refugium or bio-pellets | S6 |
| 60 gal estimate | 60-90 lb live rock | Calc from S6 |
| Light porous rock (pukani, shelf) | Less by weight, more bacterial surface per lb | S6 |
| Percent of volume | Not in source. Rock volume typically 10-20% of tank volume **[K]** | K |
| Rock density | Dense rock (aragonite-heavy) ~ 2.5 g/cc; porous rock ~ 1.5-2.0 g/cc **[K]** | K |

For a 48 x 18 x 20 in tank (~60 gal, 13.8 cu ft): 10-20% by volume = 1.4-2.8 cu ft of rock. Use this for Blender scale.

### E2. Aquascape styles

S6 does not name styles. It recommends 3D structure, gaps, arches, avoiding back-glass stacking, open swim lanes, varied sizes [S6].

| Style | Description | Use in this tank |
|---|---|---|
| Wall / back-wall | Rock stacked against back glass | Avoid; S6 warns of anaerobic zones and poor flow |
| Arches | Rock bridges with gaps | Good for 48" tank; use 2-3 arches with 3-6 in gaps |
| Islands | Free-standing rock clumps in sand | Good for flow and coral placement |
| Caves | Overhangs and grottos | Good for shade and fish; mind clove and GSP light |
| Mixed (recommended) | Low left-side wall, arch center, islands right | Matches tank proportions |

### E3. Rock texture for 3D

| Feature | Look | Blender approach [K] |
|---|---|---|
| Porosity | Holes 1-20 mm diam, irregular | Voronoi cells with random subtraction (boolean) or displacement from Voronoi F1 |
| Color | Tan/white base #D8CBB0, algae-coated #3B5B2E, coralline #D46A8A, brown #6B4A2E | Base color noise + mask |
| Texture | Rough, pitted, lumpy | Musgrave/fBm displacement, strength 0.05-0.3 of rock size |
| Shelf rock | Flat plates with ledges | Stack boxes with bevel and noise |
| Holes | Boolean holes with spheres (2-15 mm) | Scatter sphere booleans inside rock |

### E4. Procedural rock in Blender [K]

- Base shape: icosphere (subdiv 5-6) scaled to rock size (5-15 cm).
- Displacement: Musgrave (fBm) or Voronoi F1 with strength 0.05-0.15 of size; combine for pit + lump.
- Porous holes: Voronoi cell edges, threshold to make holes, or a boolean with many small spheres.
- Sand: aragonite sand look = fine grain noise, color #E6DFD0 to #C9BEA6, roughness 0.8-0.9, slight normal bump, small scale (0.2-1 mm features).

### E5. Aragonite sand

| Property | Value |

| Grain | 0.2-1.0 mm [K] |
| Depth | 1-2 in typical; 2-3 in for deep beds [K] |

### F5. Sunrise / sunset ramps and moonlight

S7 does not list schedules. Typical ramp [K]:
| Time | Phase | Intensity | Channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-1:00 | Moon | 1-5% | Moonlight |
| 1:00-2:30 | Dawn | 0 to 30% | Violet + royal blue |
| 2:30-4:00 | Ramp | 30 to 100% | Add blue, cool white |
| 4:00-9:00 | Peak | 100% | All channels |
| 9:00-10:30 | Dusk ramp | 100 to 20% | Cool white drops first |
| 10:30-12:00 | Dusk | 20 to 0% | Violet last |

For Blender: ramp light intensity 0 -> 1 -> 0 over sim time; color shifts from violet-blue (dawn) to white (noon) to blue (dusk). Moon: #2B3A6E at 1-5% strength.

### F6. Rendering the light in Blender [K]

- Light type: spot or area lights matching gooseneck mount; color temperature (Blender "Blackbody" or hex) per channel mix.
- Caustics: use a caustic texture projected from the surface (animated noise); keep low opacity 0.2-0.4.
- Light shafts: volumetric fog in water with low density 0.001-0.005 per cm; scatter color from light.
- Glass tint: slight green/blue tint in the glass using Fresnel-based transmission (#E0F8F0 at 5% strength).

## G. Water and underwater rendering tips (mobile target)

| Effect | Approach | Mobile cost |
|---|---|---|
| Caustics | Animated caustic texture (2-4 frames or scrolling noise) projected onto rock and sand, additive, 0.2-0.4 opacity | Low |
| Particulate | Sprites or small instanced quads, 20-60 particles, slow drift, alpha 0.1-0.3 | Low |
| Surface ripples from below | Normal map scrolling, Fresnel reflection to view angle; total internal reflection cone visible from below at ~48 deg **[K]** | Low-medium |
| Glass reflections | Cubemap reflection on front glass, roughness 0.1, alpha 0.05-0.1 | Low |
| Light falloff | Vertical gradient from surface, darker at bottom; blue shift with depth | Very low |
| Shadows | Single baked or cheap directional shadow | Low |
| Fog | Exponential fog, density per tank depth, tint #0E3B5A | Very low |
| Flow sway | Vertex shader sine offset, amplitude as above | Low |
