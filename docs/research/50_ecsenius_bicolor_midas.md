# Ecsenius bicolor (bicolor blenny) and Ecsenius midas (midas blenny): research for the ReefLive simulation

Scope: body and fin form, colour for 3D painting, behaviour, diet and care, reproduction, US retail prices, and proposed sim parameters for the two Ecsenius blennies. Sand-sifting and burrow mechanics belong to other notes; this file only references them.

Tags: [SOURCED: name] = a database, retailer or paper statement, quoted or closely paraphrased. [OBSERVATION: aquarium hobby report] = care guide, retailer, forum. [ANALOGY: other species] = transferred from a related species or genus. [DESIGN ESTIMATE] = my value with no source. [UNKNOWN] = searched, no source found. "calc" = derived arithmetically from a sourced number.

Limits of this pass: Springer (1988) monograph PDF returned HTTP 403, so fin-ray counts and head-cirri status could not be checked against the taxonomic source. Several FishBase and Aquariadise / Reefs.com pages also returned 403 and were used only through search snippets where marked. Prices are web snapshots of unknown date (stock and prices change); none is a verified 2025-26 average.

## 1. Bicolor blenny, Ecsenius bicolor (authority not checked; see FishBase)

### 1a. Size, body, fins, head, mouth, eyes
- [SOURCED: FishBase, fishbase.se/summary/6033] Max length 11.0 cm TL male/unsexed (Ref. 2334). Body shape recorded as "elongated". Depth 1-25 m.
- [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source, aquariumsource.com/bicolor-blenny] Average aquarium size "3 inches, not uncommon to reach 4 inches". Males larger than females.
- [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source] Females have a shorter, deeper body than males (search snippet describing males as "elongate", females "short"); treat as a sexual-dimorphism hint, not a measurement.
- Body depth ratio (depth / standard length): [DESIGN ESTIMATE] 0.18-0.20 for males, 0.22-0.25 for females. No source gave a measured ratio.
- Dorsal fin: long continuous dorsal along the back, spines and rays in one fin. Exact spine/ray counts: [UNKNOWN] (Springer 1988 not accessible).
- Caudal fin: rounded to slightly truncate. [DESIGN ESTIMATE] Not a lyretail. No source says lyretail for this species.
- Head cirri (supraorbital / nasal): [UNKNOWN]. A pink curved marking sits behind the eye on all colour forms [OBSERVATION: OzAnimals, ozanimals.com/Fish/Bicolor-Blenny/Ecsenius/bicolor.html, search snippet]. The marking is colour, not a tentacle, as far as sources say.
- Mouth: Blenniidae comb-tooth family [SOURCED: FishBase family summary via search]. Small, terminal, with incisor-like comb teeth used for scraping. Exact tooth count: [UNKNOWN].
- Eye: high on the head, anterior-dorsal. [DESIGN ESTIMATE] Only sourced description is the hobby "bright blue eyes" for midas; bicolor eye colour is not described in sources reviewed.

### 1b. Colour (for 3D painting)
- [SOURCED: FishBase] Three colour phases:
  1. Bicolor phase: black anteriorly, yellow posteriorly. The split is roughly at the dorsal fin origin or slightly behind it [DESIGN ESTIMATE for split location; FishBase gives no line].
  2. Black dorsally with a broad white lateral band and dusky belly. Tail may be yellow or not.
  3. Same as 2 but with yellow tail (per FishBase "with or without a yellow tail").
- [OBSERVATION: OzAnimals] Dark brown all over is also listed as a form. Pink curved mark behind eye on all forms.
- [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source] "blue upper half and orange-yellow lower half". Males brighter and bluer, especially when breeding. Females duller orange, the "flame".
- Breeding male [OBSERVATION: single retailer page, unidentified; search snippet]: reddish with white bars during spawning. Female light brown and yellow-orange. After spawning, male dark blue with light patches on each side. Treat the red-with-white-bars claim as unverified. Source is one retailer, not a paper.
- Juvenile colour: [UNKNOWN]. No text description found. Japanese museum database has photos of 3.4 and 3.6 cm SL specimens; colour text not read.
- Recommended paint order for the sim: front dark brown/blue-black, rear orange-yellow (morph 1), with the white-band morph as a 25% variant. Juveniles: paint like morph 1 at smaller size (design estimate).

### 1c. Behaviour
- [SOURCED: FishBase] "Found solitary in abandoned tubes." Lives in clear lagoon and seaward reefs with "mixed corals and algae-covered rocks." Solitary; pairs in breeding context ("distinct pairing").
- [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source] Described as "passive, accommodating"; perches on rocks or corals, clings to the bottom, is drawn to crevices and hiding spots.
- [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source] "Infamous jumper."
- Grazing: benthic. [SOURCED: FishBase trophic level 2.0 "based on food items"; FishBase gives no diet composition.]
- Day/night: [UNKNOWN] specific to this species. Assumed diurnal with night retreat into a hole, as for other Ecsenius [ANALOGY: tailspot blenny, doc 30 section 1; midas, Aquariadise "diurnal"].
- Territoriality: [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source] aggressive toward other algae eaters "including other Bicolor Blennies". Wild territory size and chase rate: [UNKNOWN] for this species. Genus-level [ANALOGY: Salarias fasciatus, Townsend & Tibbetts 2004, doc 30]: about 90% of entering blennies chased.
- Threat display: [ANALOGY: 1960s German film catalogue, doc 30] rising up and gaping. No frequency data.
- Flight distance / hop length: [UNKNOWN].
- Reef safety: [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source] "technically a reef safe fish" because it mainly eats algae. May nip coral "if underfed or if overcrowding causes distress".
- Jumping: yes [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source; Quality Marine, search snippet].

### 1d. Diet, care, life
- Diet: algae-based. [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source] "This fish will die without algae." Micro-algae recommended. Brine shrimp and mysis are the only meaty foods it generally eats. Reefs.com (search snippet) adds spirulina-enriched brine shrimp and marine herbivore preparations.
- Feeding frequency: [OBSERVATION: Aquariadise/Aquarium Source pattern for blennies] 2-3 times a day, small amounts. Bicolor specific: not found; treat as design estimate.
- Hardiness: [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source] "passive, accommodating", "good beginner fish" if owner commits.
- Min tank: 30 gal [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source; search snippet].
- Temperature / SG: [DESIGN ESTIMATE] 24-27 C, SG 1.023-1.025. No source fetched this pass.
- Lifespan: [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source] 2-4 years wild, up to 10 years captive. Search snippet gives 4-6 years; conflicting. Use 4-10 years for the sim.
- Care level: easy [OBSERVATION: multiple guides].

### 1e. Reproduction
- [SOURCED: FishBase] Oviparous. Eggs demersal and adhesive, anchored to the substrate by a "filamentous pad or pedestal". Larvae planktonic, often in shallow coastal water. "Distinct pairing."
- Male guards eggs in a hole: [ANALOGY: generic blenny; not sourced for this species]. Captive breeding: one profile says not known in captivity; German lexicon says offspring possible but numbers too small for trade [OBSERVATION].
- Size at maturity: [UNKNOWN].

## 2. Midas blenny, Ecsenius midas (Starck 1969)

### 2a. Size, body, fins, head, mouth, eyes
- [SOURCED: FishBase, fishbase.se/summary/Ecsenius-midas] Max length 13.0 cm TL male/unsexed. Depth 2-40 m. Common name on FishBase: "Persian blenny".
- [OBSERVATION: Quality Marine, search fetch] "Midas blennies can grow to 5 inches". Males larger than females.
- [OBSERVATION: LiveAquaria, search snippet] Max size 6 inches in listing.
- [OBSERVATION: Aquariadise] "between 4 and 6 inches long", size depends on tank, stress, diet.
- Body shape: [DESIGN ESTIMATE] more elongate than bicolor; depth ratio 0.20-0.22 for males. Reason: midas swims in water column and is compared to eels and anthias [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source search snippet, "often mimic eels"].
- Dorsal fin: long, continuous. Counts [UNKNOWN].
- Caudal fin: midas lyretail. [UNKNOWN] in sources reviewed. Hobby photos commonly show a rounded to lightly forked caudal. Lyretail is only attested for the anthias it mimics (Pseudanthias squamipinnis). Paint and model as rounded-to-lightly-forked (design estimate) until checked against Springer 1988 or a verified photo.
- Head cirri: [UNKNOWN].
- Mouth: comb-tooth, small terminal (Blenniidae) [SOURCED family].
- Eyes: "bright blue eyes" [OBSERVATION: Quality Marine]. Blue ring/accents round the eye [OBSERVATION: Aquariadise "stunning blue accents around the fins and eyes"].

### 2b. Colour (for 3D painting)
- [SOURCED: FishBase] Main form: "Golden orange in color", with a black spot around the anus.
- [SOURCED: FishBase] Indonesian form: grey or greenish to golden-yellow.
- [OBSERVATION: Aquariadise] "deep golden color, highlighted by vibrant blue flashes around the fins and eyes". Red Sea and Indian Ocean fish said to be more vibrant than Pacific fish.
- [OBSERVATION: Quality Marine] "overall orange to salmon body coloration with gold and yellow highlights".
- Blue edge on dorsal and caudal: [OBSERVATION] blue flashes on fins; paint as thin blue edge on dorsal and caudal (design estimate for exact placement).
- Blue-grey / purple morph: [UNKNOWN] in sources reviewed; the Indonesian grey/greenish form is the closest sourced alternative.
- Anal spot: black, at base of anal fin, [SOURCED: FishBase "black spot near the anus"].
- Rapid colour change: [SOURCED: FishBase] can shift colour quickly to match the fish it schools with, and mood/stress [OBSERVATION: Aquariadise "canaries of the marine reef coal mine"].
- Mimicry: [SOURCED: FishBase] orange-yellow phase resembles and schools with Pseudanthias squamipinnis; copies swimming of P. huchtii and Lepidozygus tapeinosoma (FishBase ref. 90102, not checked).

### 2c. Behaviour
- [SOURCED: FishBase] Adults swim "2 to 3 m above the benthos" feeding on plankton; zooplankton reported.
- [OBSERVATION: Aquariadise] Diurnal. Moves across reef with a swim-crawl mix, "darts in and out of crevices and caves". Roams rocks and caves looking for food.
- Hole behaviour: [OBSERVATION: ultimatereef anecdote, doc 30] a single midas guards a hole mouth and excludes others. Anecdote only.
- Territoriality: [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source] "incredibly aggressive toward other blennies". [OBSERVATION: Aquariadise] "territorially aggressive toward fish of the same species". Keep one per tank.
- Nipping: [OBSERVATION: Aquariadise] some hobbyists report nipping small gobies and firefish competing for similar food.
- Jumping: [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source; Quality Marine; Aquariadise] "Blennies have a habit of jumping when stressed". Tight lid essential.
- Reef safety: [OBSERVATION: Quality Marine] "easy and peaceful", aggressive to fish that resemble it. No coral-nipping reports found; [UNKNOWN].
- Night: [UNKNOWN]; assumed retreat into crevice [ANALOGY: tailspot, doc 30].
- Flight distance / hop length: [UNKNOWN].

### 2d. Diet, care, life
- [SOURCED: FishBase trophic level 2.6, SE 0.29, based on food items] Trophic level 2.6 is higher than bicolor's 2.0, which fits the zooplankton diet.
- Zooplankton: [OBSERVATION: Aquariadise FAQ] "Midas blennies eat copepods, zooplankton, amphipods".
- Diet mix: [OBSERVATION: Aquariadise] omnivore; commercial omnivore flakes, brine shrimp, mysis, chopped mussels and clams, spirulina, algae foods. Quality Marine: supplement with algae-based foods when algae is scarce.
- Feeding frequency: "two or three times a day, offering only what they will clear in a few minutes" [OBSERVATION: Aquariadise; Aquarium Source].
- Min tank: 30 gal [OBSERVATION: Aquariadise; Quality Marine; LiveAquaria; Aquarium Source]. 90+ gal for pairs (Aquarium Source; not recommended for sim).
- Temperature / SG: [DESIGN ESTIMATE] 24-27 C, SG 1.023-1.025.
- Lifespan: [OBSERVATION: Aquarium Source] 2-4 years. Hobby claims as high as 6 years; treat 3-6 years.
- Care level: [OBSERVATION] Quality Marine: "easy and peaceful". Aquariadise: "easy for beginners". Another blenny guide: "intermediate to advanced". Conflicting; use easy-moderate.

### 2e. Reproduction
- [SOURCED: FishBase] Oviparous. Demersal, adhesive eggs anchored by a filamentous pad or pedestal. Larvae planktonic. "Distinct pairing."
- Male guard in hole: [ANALOGY: genus; not sourced].
- Size at maturity: [UNKNOWN].
- Captive breeding: not found.

## 5. Conflicts and open items
- Bicolor max length: FishBase 11 cm vs hobby 3-4 in. Sim uses the FishBase max for max and hobby size for typical.
- Midas size: FishBase 13 cm vs hobby 4-6 in (10-15 cm). Consistent.
- Lifespan: bicolor 2-4 yr wild and 10 yr captive (Aquarium Source) vs 4-6 yr (search). Midas 2-4 yr vs up to 6 yr.
- Care level: midas "easy" vs "intermediate to advanced" (one blenny guide).
- Midas caudal shape: lyretail not confirmed. Need Springer 1988 figure or verified photo.
- Head cirri: unknown for both.
- Bicolor breeding male colour: single retailer source, unverified.
- Bicolor juvenile colour: no source.
- Midas Indonesian grey/greenish form: sourced in FishBase; blue-grey/purple morph: no source.
- Midas FishBase mimicry claim (ref. 90102): not checked.
- Bulk Reef Supply prices: not found for either species.
